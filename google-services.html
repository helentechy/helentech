The Problem

Most businesses have some Google presence (a Business Profile, maybe a product feed, maybe an Ads account) but it's often incomplete, misconfigured, or abandoned after setup.

The result: a suspended listing that no one noticed, a product feed full of disapproved items, or ad spend going toward the wrong searches.

How HelenTech Helps

We audit what's already there, fix what's broken, and set up proper tracking so you can see what's actually working.

Then we keep it maintained, because Google's requirements and your business both change over time.
